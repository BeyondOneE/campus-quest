Title
Rokid Global University AR Application Challenge (July)
Problem
Rokid, founded in 2014 in Hangzhou, China, is a pioneering tech company focused on human–computer interaction and augmented reality (AR). Recognized as a national high-tech enterprise in 2018, Rokid develops both hardware and software—most notably its AR glasses (Rokid Glass, Air, Max, and the recently launched AR Spatial) and the proprietary YodaOS operating system . The company’s R&D spans speech and natural language processing, computer vision, gesture recognition, and optical displays, enabling full-stack AR solutions for both consumer and industrial use . Rokid's AR products are now sold in over 80 countries and have earned multiple awards, helping drive its evolution from voice AI to a global AR innovator.

These competition will be based on Rokid AR Studio for product creativity and development.

In addition to the regular AR applications/games, a new track for spatial mini-programs has been added, aimed at encouraging developers to create more excellent AR applications and games with spatial interaction capabilities based on the Rokid YodaOS-Master system capabilities and platform services, Unity 3D engine, and Agora RTE real-time audio and video services through practical application development, market promotion, and substantial rewards. 

This initiative seeks to achieve technological, product, and business innovation, providing global users with richer, higher-quality, and novel experiences.
Who it's for
First-time visitors to the Xipu Taicang Campus, including new students and guests, find it difficult to quickly locate their target buildings, often getting lost and wasting time.
Direction
Our app is a multiplayer AR campus treasure-hunt game designed for XJTLU freshmen and campus visitors.
It allows users to:
- Form a team: Players join the same group using a room code.
- Choose a route: Teams select routes such as Culture Trail, Tech Discovery, or Lakeside Mystery.
- Navigate with AR: AR glasses display directions, distances, and the next checkpoint.
- Collect virtual clues: Players find AR clues around real campus buildings and collect them using gestures.
- Solve puzzles together: Different players receive different information and communicate through Agora’s real-time voice service.
- Explore the campus: Players unlock information about campus buildings, facilities, and culture.
- Earn rewards: The app records completion time, scores, achievements, and collected clues.
