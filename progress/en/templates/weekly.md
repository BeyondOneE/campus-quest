## 1. Work completed this week

Outcomes, unfinished items, daily / implementation references, actual contributions and scope of AI use.

## 2. Challenges encountered

Technical, communication or resource issues and the actions taken.

## 3. Help requested and evidence

Mentor, TA, specialist or external mentor; actual dates, questions and evidence. State honestly when no engagement occurred.

## 4. Support outcome

Whether the issue was resolved, the response received, alternatives used and evidence.

- Next week's plan and open questions: to be completed.

## 7. Formal submission and signatures

- Platform, uploader, time, submitted files and receipt: pending confirmation.
- All member signatures: supplied personally in the controlled formal copy; never generated automatically or committed to Git.
