## CQ-20261110-009 · RoNIN stop correction

The user reported continued displacement after coming to a complete stop. Previously the live tracker kept integrating whenever model speed exceeded 0.06 m/s. Codex applied a zero-velocity constraint to predictions whose timestamps fall in confirmed still intervals. The classifier uses 400 ms of linear acceleration and angular rate around each prediction centre. This preserves buffered walking estimates and resumes integration when the phone moves again.

The prior 2 m walk recording reached nearby search with both versions. Its unmodified settled segment was stable. Adding a controlled 0.2 m/s residual to that segment produced 1.20981 m of prior-version displacement over 6.017 s, versus 0 m with the revised tracker. The recording itself does not contain that injected drift. All 112 tests, the build and the trusted LAN source check passed. The updated stop behaviour needs a phone retest.
