## CQ-20261111-001 · Leaderboard and Treasure Book

The user prioritized a leaderboard, a Treasure Book and per-player collection progress on the board. Codex added six campus-themed starter cards, a signed-in progress endpoint and English views at room selection and in the room menu. The claim view links to the book and displays the credited find.

Each new round offers one shared card. The first card in a room is chosen at random and subsequent placements cycle through the catalog. A signed-in player's first successful claim in a round records a find and unlocks the card if it is new to that account. The receipt and account credit commit together in SQLite. Account progress can be viewed after room disbanding, demo reset and server restart. The board shows the first ten scored players and the current player's own rank and collection count.

All 119 tests and the build passed. Controlled claims covered the first find, guest exclusion, retry, transaction rollback, tied ranks, card rotation, a duplicate card in another round and progress after disbanding and restart. Desktop Chrome checked room selection, the room menu and both views at 320 × 500 and 390 × 780 using isolated accounts. A private database backup preceded the LAN upgrade. Existing records, the certificate and the new API and assets were checked. Physical iPhone testing is pending. [E-20261111-001](../../evidence/2026-11-11/treasure-progress-checks.json)
